What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

Tacoma Wishlist is where community members share ideas for making Tacoma and the surrounding area better. Ideas can be big or small—from safer crossings and welcoming public spaces to youth opportunities, arts, recreation and neighborhood connections.

What you can expect

  • One community idea at a time
  • Why it matters and who it could help
  • Different perspectives on feasibility and impact
  • A growing collection of community priorities
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • What would you add, improve or change in Tacoma?
  • What would make public spaces more welcoming?
  • What opportunity is missing for local young people?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.

Your voice belongs here

Help shape this segment.

Submit an idea for the Tacoma Wishlist. Tacoma Nights welcomes thoughtful ideas, guest suggestions and community perspectives. Submissions are reviewed by the production team; publication is not guaranteed.
