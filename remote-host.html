REMOTE HOST & CONTRIBUTOR APPLICATIONS

Introduce yourself. Show us your voice.

Apply to join Tacoma Nights as a remote host, segment contributor, researcher, producer or guest. You can now include a video sample so the production team can review your on-camera presence, delivery and ideas.

Accepted formats: MP4, WebM or MOV. Maximum file size: 500 MB. A short 1–3 minute horizontal sample is recommended. Your video is uploaded to private storage and is only available to authorized reviewers.

No video selected. You may still submit your application without a video.

Applications and video samples are submitted privately for production-team review. The team may contact you using the email address provided. Please do not upload confidential, sensitive or third-party material without permission.

What makes a strong sample?

  • Introduce yourself and the topics you care about
  • Speak naturally, as if talking with the audience
  • Show a short sample segment or story idea
  • Record horizontally in a quiet, well-lit space when possible
  • Keep the file concise and under 500 MB

What happens after submission?

Your application and any video are added to the private review queue. The production team evaluates role fit, communication, reliability, topic alignment and technical readiness. If a sample is a fit, the team may contact you about next steps.

Current opportunity status

These are volunteer/community collaboration opportunities today. Future paid roles are possible as CrowRules grows, but no compensation is currently available or promised.
