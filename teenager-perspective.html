A generation in focus

Not just talked about.
Heard.

The Teenager Perspective brings the experiences, questions, ideas and opinions of teenagers to the conversation. From school and friendships to technology, sports, creativity, family, goals and the pressure of growing up, this is a place for honest conversations without adults speaking for young people.

Built around the voices of the next generation, the segment aims to connect perspectives, encourage understanding and remind everyone that young people are part of the community right now—not just its future.

This week's conversation starter

“What is one thing you wish adults understood about being a teenager today?”

A question to get the conversation started—not a test, and not a one-size-fits-all answer. Every teenager has a different story.
