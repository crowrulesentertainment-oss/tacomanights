What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

The Teenager Perspective makes room for younger voices to talk about the world they are growing up in. Topics can include school, friendships, technology, sports, creativity, opportunity, local life and what adults may be missing.

What you can expect

  • Youth-led opinions and reactions
  • Questions about school, community and everyday life
  • Intergenerational conversations when appropriate
  • A supportive, respectful space for young contributors
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • What do adults misunderstand about teenagers today?
  • What would make your school or community better?
  • What gives young people hope about the future?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.

Youth contributions are handled with appropriate consent, privacy and safeguarding. Personal contact details and sensitive information are never part of a public submission.
