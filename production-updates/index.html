THE NEXT CHAPTER

We're pausing production. Not the vision.

Our final Tacoma Nights episode of 2026 is scheduled for Friday, November 27, the day after Thanksgiving. After that episode, regular Tacoma Nights production will pause through the holiday season while we reorganize, plan and prepare for the next chapter.

This is not the end of Tacoma Nights. The break is a chance to plan ahead, improve the production process, coordinate contributors, refresh the website and return with a schedule we can maintain. The planned official return is Friday, January 15, 2027.

THE NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCTION STANDARD

Finish every episode seven days early.

We want to stop producing against the premiere-day deadline. Each Friday episode should be finished and waiting in the schedule while the team is already preparing the next one.

Every episode must be fully recorded, edited, reviewed, approved, uploaded and scheduled at least seven days before its Friday premiere.

The previous Friday is the deadline—not the day to begin final editing.

14 DAYS OUT

Plan the episode

Choose the topic, identify guests and remote hosts, assign segments, and begin research.

10–9 DAYS OUT

Prepare the content

Finish outlines, discussion questions, story research and recording plans. Confirm each contributor.

8–7 DAYS OUT

Record and collect

Record main host portions and receive all remote-host recordings, graphics and segment materials.

7 DAYS OUT

Final delivery deadline

Complete the edit, review the full episode, make corrections, approve the master, upload it and schedule the premiere.

6–3 DAYS OUT

Promote and prepare

Prepare the thumbnail, title, description, Discord and social announcements. Check the scheduled premiere.

2–1 DAYS OUT

Protect the buffer

Handle only final checks and promotion. Keep the completed episode ready; focus production time on the following week.

FRIDAY

Premiere day

Publish the already-scheduled episode, join the premiere chat and connect with the audience—without last-minute editing.

IN PARALLEL

Next episode begins

While this week's episode is ready to publish, research and planning are already underway for the next Friday.
