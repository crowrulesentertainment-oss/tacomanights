What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

Tacoma Then & Now connects the city's past with the place it is becoming. Through photographs, memories, local knowledge and conversations across generations, the segment explores how neighborhoods, landmarks, traditions and everyday life have changed.

What you can expect

  • Personal memories and oral-history conversations
  • Then-and-now locations and neighborhood stories
  • Historic photographs and community-submitted material
  • How the past shapes Tacoma's identity today
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • What Tacoma place brings back the strongest memories?
  • What has changed for the better—or worse—in your neighborhood?
  • What local story should never be forgotten?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.
