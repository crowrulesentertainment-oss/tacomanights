What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

The People's Perspective puts everyday people at the center of the conversation. Each feature gives a community member room to share a personal experience, opinion, memory or response to the question of the week.

What you can expect

  • A weekly community question or prompt
  • Short recorded video responses and occasional longer conversations
  • A range of voices, experiences and viewpoints
  • A respectful edit that keeps each contributor's meaning intact
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • What makes a community strong?
  • What is one thing you wish more people understood about Tacoma?
  • Who made a difference in your life or neighborhood?
  • What is one change that would make the 253 better?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.

Your voice belongs here

Help shape this segment.

Share your answer to the current community question. Tacoma Nights welcomes thoughtful ideas, guest suggestions and community perspectives. Submissions are reviewed by the production team; publication is not guaranteed.
