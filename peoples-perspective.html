How video submissions work
This page currently collects video links by email rather than uploading files directly. Set your link so CrowRules can view it, then submit the form. Videos are not published automatically — each submission is reviewed first.

The form will open your email app with your submission details ready to send.

A few ground rules

Keep it real. Keep it respectful.

Before you submit: Do not share private addresses, phone numbers, confidential information, threats, harassment or footage you do not have rights to use. Avoid copyrighted music in the background. If anyone under 18 appears in your video, a parent or legal guardian must provide permission. Do not film or identify someone in a private or sensitive situation without their consent. CrowRules may decline, edit for length or remove a submission.
