MAKE YOUR VIDEO COUNT

Keep it real. Keep it vertical.

A polished studio is not required. We want to hear you clearly and understand what you want to share.

  • Hold your phone vertically (9:16).
  • Frame your face comfortably and look toward the camera.
  • Choose a quiet place with good natural light.
  • Speak clearly; 30–60 seconds is a great target.
  • Answer the question in your own words—there is no scripted response.
  • Only include people who have agreed to be filmed.
