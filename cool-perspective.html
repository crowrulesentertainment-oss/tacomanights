What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

The Cool Perspective is the space for an unexpected angle, a distinct personality or an independent take. It can be funny, thoughtful, creative, curious or challenging—as long as it brings something original to the conversation.

What you can expect

  • Independent commentary and personal takes
  • Culture, entertainment, trends and everyday observations
  • Guest perspectives and creative experiments
  • Room for emerging voices and new recurring contributors
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • What is getting too much attention—and what deserves more?
  • What is one thing you see differently from everyone else?
  • What local trend should people pay attention to?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.

Your voice belongs here

Help shape this segment.

Bring a fresh idea or point of view to the show. Tacoma Nights welcomes thoughtful ideas, guest suggestions and community perspectives. Submissions are reviewed by the production team; publication is not guaranteed.
