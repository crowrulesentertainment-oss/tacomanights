Teen Submissions: Privacy & Safety

Young people deserve to be heard without having to give up their privacy. This page explains how the Teen Video Studio works and what to expect before recording or sharing anything with Tacoma Nights.

Important: the studio does not upload or publish videos.
Recording and preview happen in your browser. Clips appear only in the temporary video wall on the device and browser tab where they were recorded. They are not sent to Tacoma Nights by this tool. Downloading a clip saves a copy to your device.

1. Who this studio is for

The Teen Video Studio is designed for teenage participants. The age checkbox is an acknowledgement, not identity verification or a secure age gate. A parent or guardian should review participation with younger teens, and permission must be obtained wherever required by law or by a school, organization or activity.

2. What happens to your recording

  • Your browser asks for camera and microphone permission only when you choose to start the studio.
  • The camera stream and recording are processed locally in your browser using your device.
  • There is no automatic upload, public posting, cloud storage or submission to a Tacoma Nights account from this page.
  • The video wall is a temporary preview of clips made during the current page session. Reloading or closing the page clears that wall.
  • If you download a clip, the downloaded file is stored on your device. You control what happens to that copy.

3. Before you record

  • Do not show or say your full name, home address, phone number, email, school name, class schedule, live location, passwords or other identifying details.
  • Check the background for school logos, house numbers, mail, other people, screens or anything that reveals where you are.
  • Do not record another person unless they understand what is being recorded and have agreed. Get parent or guardian permission where required.
  • Do not include private conversations, copyrighted material you do not have permission to use, or anything intended to bully, threaten or embarrass someone.
  • Never feel pressured to participate. You can stop recording and leave at any time.

4. If you choose to share a clip later

This studio currently provides local recording and downloading only. It does not provide an upload or publishing function. If Tacoma Nights offers a separate submission route in the future, that route should explain who can access a submission, how moderation works, whether guardian consent is required, how long material is retained and how to request removal before a clip is sent.

Do not send a recording through a personal message or public post unless you and your parent or guardian have reviewed the destination and understand its rules. Submission does not guarantee publication. Any future public feature should be moderated before content appears publicly.

5. Moderation and safety

Tacoma Nights may decline material that exposes private information, targets or harasses someone, contains unsafe or inappropriate content, or does not meet the segment's standards. Do not use the studio to report an emergency. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult.

6. Questions or privacy requests

For questions about the Teenager Perspective or a privacy concern, ask a parent or guardian to contact CrowRules Entertainment at crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com. Do not include sensitive personal details in your first message.
