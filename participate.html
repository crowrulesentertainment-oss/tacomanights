How it starts

One idea can start a conversation.

Tell us what interests you, what you might bring to the show, and how you would like to take part. The production team will review submissions and contact people when there is a potential next step.

  • Choose the role or contribution that interests you
  • Share your idea, background, or community connection
  • Discuss availability and the format that makes sense
  • Follow production guidance for any recording or upload
  • Work with the team on preparation and scheduling

Submitting an application, pitch, or video does not guarantee selection, an appearance, or publication.

Important opportunity details

Independent, community-minded collaboration.

Tacoma Nights is an independent production being built from the ground up as part of CrowRules Entertainment.

Volunteer opportunity — not currently paid

Current contributor opportunities are volunteer/community collaborations, not paid positions. Future paid roles may be considered if CrowRules Entertainment grows and becomes financially able, but compensation is not guaranteed. Participation is voluntary and subject to production review, scheduling, and mutual agreement. Contact details and private submissions are intended for authorized production review and should not be treated as public content.
