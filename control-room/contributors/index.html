04 / PEOPLE FIRST

Build a better crew.

Consistent decisions create a stronger, safer creative network.

01

REVIEW THE PERSON

Read the complete application. Consider the applicant's interests, experience, availability and the contribution they want to make.

02

SET CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Explain the role, responsibilities, communication channels and whether the opportunity is currently volunteer-based.

03

GRANT LEAST ACCESS

Contributor membership is not an administrator or editor role. Keep elevated permissions limited to trusted staff.

04

KEEP PEOPLE INFORMED

Record decisions accurately and follow up respectfully. Application approval does not automatically create an account or send an invitation.
