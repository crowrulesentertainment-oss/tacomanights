03 / EDITORIAL CARE

Review with purpose.

Simple principles for handling every community voice.

01

READ IN FULL

Understand the context before making a decision. Don't judge a submission by its title alone.

02

PROTECT PRIVACY

Contact details are for staff follow-up. Do not copy private information into public content.

03

VERIFY & RESPECT

Check claims, respect consent and avoid publishing personal allegations or identifying details without appropriate review.

04

KEEP A CLEAR STATUS

Use In Review when more work is needed. Approve only when the submission is ready for the next publishing step.
