Kenneth WarnerShook hosts The Weekly Lineup. Soda & Sports United (SSU), founded by Kenneth WarnerShook, is an independent sports media startup with a passion for Washington sports, authentic fan perspectives, and meaningful community connections. From MLB, NFL, and NHL to NCAA athletics and beyond, SSU is working to bring fans closer to the teams, stories, and communities they love, with a special focus on Washington's sports landscape. The platform may be small today, but the vision is anything but. With ambitions to expand sports coverage, develop original programming, welcome aspiring contributors, and build lasting community partnerships, SSU is creating something designed to grow alongside the people who help make it possible. More than just scores and highlights, SSU believes sports are about the people, relationships, and shared experiences that bring communities together. Starting small. Dreaming big. Building together. Family, Community, Connections.
