What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

The Neighbor's Mic is a quick, welcoming microphone for the people who make up the community. Short interviews capture first impressions, personal stories, neighborhood opinions and the small moments that make a place feel connected.

What you can expect

  • Quick street-style or remote interviews
  • One clear question and concise answers
  • A mix of light, thoughtful and topical prompts
  • Community voices from across Tacoma and Pierce County
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • What do you love about your neighborhood?
  • What is one thing that made you smile this week?
  • What advice would you give someone new to Tacoma?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.

Your voice belongs here

Help shape this segment.

Volunteer for a short interview or suggest a question. Tacoma Nights welcomes thoughtful ideas, guest suggestions and community perspectives. Submissions are reviewed by the production team; publication is not guaranteed.
