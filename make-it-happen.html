What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

Make It Happen follows the next step after an idea is shared. The segment explores what it would take to move a community suggestion forward, who needs to be involved, what resources may help and how progress can be made visible.

What you can expect

  • Select an idea or local challenge
  • Identify people and groups who may help
  • Break the idea into practical next steps
  • Share progress, lessons and opportunities to participate
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • Which Tacoma Wishlist idea could we start working on?
  • What is one achievable first step?
  • Who should be part of making this happen?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.
