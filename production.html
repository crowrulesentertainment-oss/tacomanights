Production workflow

  1. Producer creates an episode brief and assigns segment contributions.
  2. Contributor opens their individual segment studio and records a 16:9 video.
  3. Contributor reviews and downloads the recording.
  4. Contributor sends the file through the approved production handoff channel.
  5. Producer reviews audio, framing, permissions and editorial accuracy.
  6. Approved content is edited and scheduled for CrowRules TV.

This release provides the public-facing workflow and local recording. A private upload/review queue is intentionally not connected to an unverified storage bucket or database schema.
