# Network operations manual

## Before broadcast
Confirm the selected encoder, YouTube destination, scenes, audio, local recording, disk space, bridge health, rundown and standby media.

## During broadcast
Monitor YouTube health and local encoder statistics. Record locally. Keep a producer on handoffs and incident notes. Do not switch engines without a rehearsed transition.

## Recovery
On host disconnect, remove the source and take standby. On media failure, skip to the next approved item and log the fault. On encoder failure, use the standby encoder only after verifying ingest configuration. Reconcile local recordings and YouTube archives after each session.
