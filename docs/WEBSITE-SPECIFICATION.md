# Tacoma Nights Website Specification

## Approved direction
Hybrid television network + community storytelling platform. Cinematic hybrid homepage, full community participation, expanded 24-workspace Command Center, and geographic reach from Tacoma outward to worldwide.

## Brand
Tagline: The City Comes Alive. Colors: black, charcoal, crimson, white and metallic gold. Editorial voice: confident, welcoming, community-minded. Realistic Tacoma/PNW imagery; no host portrait required. YouTube-first playback; support both 16:9 landscape and 9:16 vertical.

## Sitemap
Home; Watch; Shows/Segments; Community Voices; Weekly Question; Explore (Tacoma, PNW, beyond); Participate (contributors, guests, clips, topic pitches); About; Members (optional sign-in/dashboard); Admin (Command Center and workspaces).

## Homepage
Cinematic hero; featured playlist/episode; recurring segment lineup; People's Perspective; weekly question; Community in Action; 253 Spotlight; Tacoma-to-world geographic story; contributor invitation; CrowRules footer.

## Segments
The People's Perspective; The Weekly Lineup; The Teenager Perspective; The Cool Perspective; Community in Action; 253 Spotlight; Tacoma Then & Now; The Neighbor's Mic; Tacoma Wishlist; Make It Happen; The Unfiltered Question.

## Production rhythm
Saturday idea capture; Sunday choose topic/outline/guests; Monday research; Tuesday editorial; Wednesday contributor deadline and host recording; Thursday edit, review, approve and schedule; Friday promote and premiere.

## Community policy
Public submissions are reviewed before publication. Public forms require contact consent and write only to the existing submissions table. Never expose submitter emails, internal notes or unpublished stories.
