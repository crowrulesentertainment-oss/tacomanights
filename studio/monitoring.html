Recovery order

  1. Check that OBS or Streamlabs Desktop is open on the broadcast computer.
  2. Check the local bridge terminal is running and open the local health page.
  3. Reconnect the bridge from Master Control or Producer Desk.
  4. Confirm the correct scene and audio meters in the encoder.
  5. Check YouTube Studio's Live Control Room for incoming video/audio and broadcast state.
