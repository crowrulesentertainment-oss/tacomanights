Creator & Host

Jonathan Riehle

Jonathan Riehle is the creator and host of Tacoma Nights, a community-focused show exploring life in Tacoma and the Pacific Northwest through conversations about local stories, sports, business, personal experiences, and the people who make the community unique. As the founder of CrowRules Entertainment, Jonathan is building an independent entertainment company from the ground up, driven by creativity, determination, and a vision for bringing original ideas to life. Through Tacoma Nights, he hopes to create a space where different perspectives can be heard, meaningful conversations can happen, and local voices have an opportunity to reach a wider audience. From behind the microphone to behind the scenes, Jonathan is committed to growing the show, connecting with the community, and building something that can become bigger than one person. One company. One universe.
