What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

The Unfiltered Question asks one direct question and makes room for an honest, thoughtful conversation. Topics may touch on everyday life, belonging, opportunity, local issues, relationships, change and the questions people do not always get to discuss.

What you can expect

  • One central question to frame each conversation
  • Multiple viewpoints and lived experiences
  • Curiosity and respectful disagreement
  • Context and follow-up rather than easy answers
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • What does a strong community owe its neighbors?
  • Are we listening to people we disagree with?
  • What is one difficult conversation Tacoma needs to have?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.
