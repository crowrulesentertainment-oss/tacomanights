What this feature is about

More voices. More perspectives.

Community in Action highlights people, volunteer groups, nonprofits, neighborhood projects and organizations doing meaningful work. The focus is on what they do, who they serve, the challenges they face and how neighbors can get involved.

What you can expect

  • A featured person, project or organization
  • The need or opportunity being addressed
  • Real examples of community impact
  • Practical ways to participate, volunteer or learn more
Conversation starters

Questions we might explore

  • Who is doing good work that more people should know about?
  • What local project deserves more support?
  • Where can neighbors make a difference together?

Topics and formats can evolve with the community and the people who contribute.
