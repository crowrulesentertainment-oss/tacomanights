Our purpose

The city has a voice.

Starting in Tacoma and Pierce County, Tacoma Nights creates room for conversations about everyday life, community, business, sports, entertainment and the experiences that connect us.

As the show grows, the name stays rooted in Tacoma while the perspectives and contributors can reach far beyond the city.
